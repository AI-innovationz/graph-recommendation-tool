Medicines are important for treating illnesses and saving lives during emergencies. Some medicines, known as life-saving medicines, must be available without delay because they are used to treat serious conditions such as heart attacks, severe allergic reactions, infections, asthma attacks, or uncontrolled bleeding. During disasters or medical emergencies, hospitals, pharmacies, ambulances, and relief organizations work together to ensure that essential medicines reach patients as quickly as possible. Community volunteers may help by locating nearby pharmacies, transporting medicines to people who cannot travel, or assisting elderly and disabled individuals in obtaining their prescribed medicines. Timely access to emergency medicines can prevent complications, reduce suffering, and save lives.

Shortness of breath means difficulty breathing.

Exercise can cause shortness of breath.

Asthma can cause breathing problems.

Anxiety can cause breathing problems.

Allergies can cause breathing problems.

Lung infections can cause breathing problems.

Heart problems can cause breathing problems.

Some medicines can cause breathing problems.

Smoking can cause breathing problems.

Call emergency services if you have severe breathing problems.

Call emergency services if you have chest pain.

Call emergency services if you faint.

Contact a doctor if breathing problems continue.

Contact a doctor if breathing problems get worse.

Contact a doctor if breathing problems affect daily activities.
